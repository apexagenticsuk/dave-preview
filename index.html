About Dave

Dave Young is an Irish comedian. He gained notoriety for having one of the craziest comedy shows in the country and has been honoured with numerous industry accolades, including the Performing Arts Award for Outstanding Contribution to Comedy.

His first big break came at Dublin’s Olympia Theatre, where he was selected from hundreds of audition hopefuls, the start of many stage and TV appearances throughout Europe.

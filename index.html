About Dave

Award-winning Irish comedian, singer, entertainer and all-round agent of chaos, Dave Young has built a reputation for delivering one of the wildest comedy shows in the country. With a Performing Arts Award for Outstanding Contribution to Comedy under his belt, Dave is proof that making a living out of talking nonsense can be a very serious business!

His big break came at Dublin’s legendary Olympia Theatre, launching a career spanning live theatre, television, music and film.

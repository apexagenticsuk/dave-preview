Where do I get a key?

Your web person sets this up with you once. In short: sign in to github.com, go to Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token. Set Resource owner to apexagenticsuk, pick Only select repositories → dave-preview, and under Repository permissions set Contents to Read and write. Copy the key and paste it above.

How to edit your website

Sign in

Open this page, paste your editor key and tap Sign in. You only do this once on each phone or computer. Demo mode lets you try everything without changing the real site.

Change words

Pick a page at the top. Things you can change get a yellow outline when you point at them. Click a heading, paragraph or button and just type. Click somewhere else when you're done.

Change a button's link

Click the button, then tap 🔗 Link in the little bar that appears and paste the new web address (or a phone number).

Change a show date

Click a show in the dates list to change its date, venue, town or ticket link. Use Sold out on the row when it sells out, drag ⠿ to change the order, ✕ to delete, and + Add show under the list for a new one.

Swap the poster or a photo

Click any picture and choose Replace picture. Photos from your phone are shrunk automatically.

Move or hide a section

Click inside a section and use Section ▲ ▼ to move it, or Hide. Hidden sections stay faded in the editor so you can bring them back. For repeated things like testimonials, clips or shop items use ⧉ Copy and 🗑.

Check, then publish

📱 Phone shows how it looks on a mobile. Preview opens the site with your changes. When you're happy, tap the big red Publish. The real website updates in about a minute.

Made a mistake?

Use ↶ Undo. Unpublished changes are kept even if you close the page. Every published version is saved, so your web person can always roll back.
